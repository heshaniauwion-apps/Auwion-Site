Where SEO fields live

Rather than a separate SEO section, each page and article carries its own seo_title and seo_description fields directly — this keeps the meta description next to the content it describes, which is easier to keep accurate than managing it separately.

Once the Pages and Articles editors are built (next step), an SEO section will appear directly inside each page/article's edit screen.
